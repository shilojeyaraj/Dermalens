# Dermalens Demo Video Script (3 Minutes)

## **Introduction (0:00 - 0:30)**
- **Hook**: "Meet Dermalens - the AI-powered skincare assistant that analyzes your skin and provides personalized recommendations using Google Cloud and Elasticsearch"
- **Problem**: "Traditional skincare advice is generic and doesn't consider your unique skin conditions"
- **Solution**: "Dermalens uses computer vision, machine learning, and intelligent search to give you personalized skincare recommendations"

## **Live Demo (0:30 - 2:30)**

- Show signup process

- Highlight: "We collect comprehensive data about your skin type, concerns, and preferences"

### **2. Face Scan & AI Analysis (0:45 - 1:30)**

- **AI Analysis**: "Our PyTorch model analyzes your skin for 12 different conditions including acne, hyperpigmentation, and aging signs"

- **Intelligent Search**: "We use Elasticsearch to search through thousands of skincare products"
- **Personalized Results**: Show product recommendations based on detected conditions
- **Smart Filtering**: Demonstrate filtering by skin type, price range, and preferences
- **Real-time Data**: "Our Fivetran connector keeps product data fresh and up-to-date"
