# LinkedIn Post - Dermalens Project

🚀 **Excited to share my latest project: Dermalens - An AI-Powered Skincare Analysis Platform!** 🌟

After weeks of development, I'm thrilled to announce the launch of Dermalens, a comprehensive skincare analysis platform that combines cutting-edge AI technology with intelligent product recommendations.

**What is Dermalens?**
Dermalens is an innovative skincare platform that uses advanced computer vision and AI to analyze your skin health and provide personalized product recommendations. Simply upload photos from multiple angles, and our AI-powered system delivers detailed skin analysis with actionable insights.

**Key Features:**
✅ **Multi-Angle Face Scanning** - Advanced face detection with real-time feedback
✅ **AI-Powered Analysis** - Powered by Google Gemini 1.5 Pro for medical-grade skin analysis
✅ **Intelligent Product Search** - Elasticsearch-powered search with 50+ personalized recommendations
✅ **Smart Filtering** - Real-time brand and price filtering for products
✅ **Personalized Routines** - AI-generated morning and evening skincare routines
✅ **User Profiles** - Save and manage your skin profile for ongoing recommendations

**Tech Stack:**
🔹 Frontend: Next.js 14, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Radix UI
🔹 Backend: FastAPI (Python), Supabase (PostgreSQL + Auth)
🔹 AI: Google Gemini 1.5 Pro, Vertex AI
🔹 Search: Elasticsearch for intelligent product discovery
🔹 Cloud: Google Cloud Platform (Cloud Run, BigQuery)
🔹 Infrastructure: Docker, CI/CD with Cloud Build

**The Challenge:**
Building a production-ready AI application that provides accurate, personalized skincare recommendations while maintaining excellent user experience and performance. From handling ambiguous database queries to implementing real-time filters and managing complex state, every feature was built with scalability and reliability in mind.

**What I Learned:**
- Advanced AI integration with Google Cloud services
- Real-time data synchronization between frontend and backend
- Building intuitive UI/UX for complex AI workflows
- Production deployment and optimization strategies
- Working with Elasticsearch for intelligent search capabilities

**The Result:**
A fully functional, production-ready application that delivers personalized skincare analysis with a beautiful, modern interface. Every feature - from authentication to product recommendations - is working seamlessly in production.

I'm incredibly proud of what we've built, and excited to see how it helps people discover better skincare solutions! 💆‍♀️✨
